Career quiz FAQ

Quick answers before you begin.

How long does the career quiz take?

Most people should be able to complete the 12-question assessment in around two minutes.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The assessment is designed to work without registration or an email address.

Is this a scientifically validated career test?

This version is a career-exploration and self-reflection tool, not a clinical or professionally validated psychological assessment.

Can the result tell me exactly what career I should choose?

No. A quiz can offer ideas, but career decisions also depend on your interests, skills, education, experience, goals, and real-world opportunities.

Can I retake the assessment?

Yes. Use the Retake Assessment button on the result screen to start again.
