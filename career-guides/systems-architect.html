Systems Architect

Systems Architects enjoy understanding how different parts work together, solving complex problems, and creating structured solutions.

Core Strengths

  • Analytical thinking
  • Systems thinking
  • Problem solving
  • Precision
  • Logical reasoning

Career Directions

People with this working style may enjoy areas such as software and technology, data and analytics, engineering, systems design, cybersecurity, and operations.
